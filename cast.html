Roles We're Looking For

Actors

Lead and supporting roles. All ages and experience levels welcome. Prepare a short monologue if invited to audition.

Cinematographer

Experienced in short film cinematography. Must have own equipment or access to rental gear.

Sound Engineer

On-set sound recording and post-production audio mixing. Boom operation experience preferred.

Production Assistant

General production support. Great opportunity for those new to film production to gain experience.

Makeup & Wardrobe

Special effects makeup experience a plus. Horror/gore makeup skills highly desired.

Editor

Post-production video editing. Proficiency in Premiere Pro or DaVinci Resolve required.
