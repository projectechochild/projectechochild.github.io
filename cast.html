Characters

Click on a character name to reveal details. Click "Audition" to apply for that role.

01

Jesse

The Sentimental Soul

Age: 12 ▼

At twelve years old, Jesse is the quiet heartbeat of the family. She is a sensitive child who navigates the world through her heart, often found clutching a faded polaroid or an old trinket that reminds her of better days. Living in the humid stretch of Louisiana, she feels the weight of her parents' fracturing relationship more than she lets on. She is the type to insist on "doing the right thing," even when the world around her feels morally gray. Her fairness and innocence contrast sharply with the darkening shadows of her home.

02

Shasha

The Fading Mother

Age: 30 ▼

Shasha is a woman standing at 5'3" who seems to be shrinking under the weight of a heavy, unspoken depression. At 30, the vitality of her youth is being replaced by a weary grayness; her hair, once vibrant, has begun to show dark, patchy spots—perhaps a physical manifestation of her internal decay. She spends a lot of time behind the wheel of her weathered Nissan, the engine's hum a backdrop to her silence. Having built her life and motherhood in Louisiana, she now looks like a stranger in her own skin.

03

David

The Restless Father

Age: 30 ▼

Standing 5'9" with a rugged, white complexion, David carries the marks of a turbulent past—most notably a prison tattoo etched across his chest that serves as a permanent reminder of time served. At 30, he is a man of intense, singular cravings; currently, he is gripped by an almost primal hunger for homemade food, a yearning for the domestic comfort he likely lacked for years. Though he is Jesse's father, there is a lingering edge to him that suggests he is still trying to figure out how to be a "home" for anyone.

04

The Woman with the Veil

The Mourning Daughter

Age: 30 ▼

Though she is only 30, grief has aged her like a decade of hard winters. The daughter of a legendary fighter, she carries herself with a gentle, fragile grace that seems at odds with her father's violent legacy. Having recently lost her own daughter, she hides her face behind a veil, creating a barrier between her sorrow and the world. She is a portrait of profound loss—soft-spoken and ethereal, yet anchored by a pain that makes her look much older than the woman Shasha used to know.

05

Tom "The Beast" Wilmington

The Departed Legend

Age: 30 (flashbacks) ▼

Known in every corner of the state as the "Iron Fist of Louisiana," Tom was a titan of the boxing ring whose presence was once deafening. Though he passed away a few months ago, his absence is louder than his life. His bedroom remains a shrine of white-sheeted furniture, smelling of dust and old leather. However, there is a heavy, static energy left behind in that room. Among the covered chairs and shadows, something lingers—something that hasn't realized the legend is dead, and it certainly isn't made of fabric.
