About Echo Child

Synopsis

In a torrential Louisiana rainstorm, Shasha drives with her twelve years old daughter Jesse and her husband David after a pick up on the road. A falling tree blocks the road, forcing the family to shelter in the isolated home of the late boxing champion Tom Wilmington. Inside, horrors erupt — the reanimated corpse of Tom attacks with a sledgehammer, and a disturbing dollhouse holds dark secrets. All horrors lead back to a meat freezer that safe guarded by the daughter of Wilmington who lost her daughter not so long ago. While a mysterious government spy is taken shelter near by to seek a lost government project called Echo Child at any cost.

Director's Statement

"Echo Child is born from my own fascination with how places hold onto our memories — sometimes tighter than we hold onto them ourselves. I wanted to create a film that doesn't rely on jump scares, but instead builds a lingering sense of dread that stays with the viewer long after the credits roll."

— Director, Echo Child
