The Fading Mother

At 30, Shasha is a woman standing at 5'3" who seems to be shrinking under the weight of a heavy depression. Her hair, once vibrant, has begun to show dark, patchy spots. She spends time behind the wheel of her weathered Nissan, the engine's hum a backdrop to her silence.
