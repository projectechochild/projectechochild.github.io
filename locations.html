Locations Needed

Echo Child requires several distinct locations to bring its haunting narrative to life. If you know of or have access to any of these spaces, please let us know.

Abandoned House

A dilapidated house with peeling wallpaper, creaky floors, and an eerie atmosphere. Interior and exterior shots needed.

Needed

Dark Forest / Woods

Dense woodland with minimal light penetration. Preferably with a small clearing for key scenes.

Needed

Basement / Cellar

A damp, dimly lit basement or cellar with stone walls. Must be accessible for lighting equipment.

Needed

School Hallway

An empty school hallway, preferably with lockers. Evening/weekend access required.

Needed

Urban Alleyway

A narrow alleyway with graffiti and minimal foot traffic. Night shooting required.

Needed

Small Theater

A small auditorium or theater space for the film's climax scene. Seating for 50+ preferred.
