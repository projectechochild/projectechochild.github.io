About Echo Child

Echo Child is a haunting short film set deep in the Louisiana backcountry. When a fallen tree blocks the road during a relentless rainstorm, a fractured family—Shasha, a hollowed-out mother grappling with clinical depression; David, her ex-con husband marked by a prison tattoo and desperate for domestic peace; and their 12-year-old daughter Jesse, who hears echoes from the walls—seeks shelter in the decaying mansion of boxing legend Tom "The Beast" Wilmington.

Inside, the family discovers that the Beast's legacy is far from buried. Tom himself still lingers—a reanimated corpse that stalks the halls wielding a sledgehammer. A mysterious dollhouse seems to echo the mansion's darkest secrets, and a locked meat freezer hums behind a door guarded by a woman in a veil: The Veil, Tom's grieving daughter, who lost her own child and now protects whatever lies inside.

As Jesse's connection to the echoes deepens, the truth behind the Echo Child project begins to surface. But someone is watching from the shadows—a government spy with a vested interest in the family and the secrets the Wilmington mansion was meant to bury.

Our Vision

We aim to create a cinematic experience that blends psychological horror with intimate family drama. The film will use innovative sound design, practical effects, and atmospheric cinematography to create a world where the line between reality and nightmare blur.
