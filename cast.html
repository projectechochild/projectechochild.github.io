Join the Cast

We're looking for passionate actors and crew members to bring Echo Child to life. All experience levels welcome. Click on a character to learn more.

Jesse

Protagonist - 12 years old

Age 12 ▼

Jesse is a soulful 12-year-old with a short bob cut, fair skin, and a fluent English accent. She speaks in whispers and hears echoes from the walls—voices from a past she cannot remember. Beneath her quiet vulnerability lies a depth of emotion that makes her the unexpected bridge between two worlds.

Shasha

Mother of Jesse - 30 years old

Age 30 ▼

Shasha is a hollowed-out 30-year-old mother fighting clinical depression. At 5'3", she drives a weathered Nissan and carries an exhaustion that transcends language. English fluency is optional for this role—her isolation and "otherness" speak louder than words ever could.

David

Father of Jesse - 30 years old

Age 30 ▼

David is a rugged 30-year-old ex-con with a prison chest tattoo and a desperate craving for domestic peace. At 5'9", he projects a hardened exterior that masks a man who just wants to keep his family together. Must be comfortable appearing shirtless or wearing a prosthetic tattoo.

The Woman with the Veil

Tom's Daughter - 35 years old

Age 35 ▼

The Veil is Tom Wilmington's daughter, a weathered woman in her mid-30s grieving the loss of her own child. Fair-skinned with a prematurely aged look, she speaks with a melodic, haunting quality and possesses a great singing voice. She guards the meat freezer in her father's mansion, protecting whatever secrets lie within.

Tom "The Beast" Wilmington

Deceased Boxing Champion - Late 50s

Late 50s ▼

Tom Wilmington is the late heavyweight boxing champion known as "The Iron Fist of Louisiana." Standing 6'2" with a massive, imposing frame and a prominent stomach, he was a force of nature in and out of the ring. Though deceased, his presence lingers in the walls of his decaying mansion—and in something far less human.
