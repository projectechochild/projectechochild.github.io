About Echo Child

Synopsis

When a young woman returns to her childhood home after her mother's mysterious disappearance, she discovers that the house holds memories that refuse to stay buried. As echoes from the past grow louder, she must confront the truth about what really happened — and who, or what, still lingers in the shadows.

Echo Child is a psychological horror short film that blends atmospheric tension with deeply personal storytelling. Drawing inspiration from classic ghost stories and modern horror, the film explores themes of memory, guilt, and the inescapable echoes of our past.

Director's Statement

"Echo Child is born from my own fascination with how places hold onto our memories — sometimes tighter than we hold onto them ourselves. I wanted to create a film that doesn't rely on jump scares, but instead builds a lingering sense of dread that stays with the viewer long after the credits roll."

— Director, Echo Child
