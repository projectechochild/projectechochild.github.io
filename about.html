About Echo Child

Echo Child is a haunting short film that explores the echoes of the past and the shadows they cast on the present. Set against the backdrop of a Louisiana rainstorm, the story follows Jesse, a quiet 8-year-old who speaks only in whispers and claims to hear voices from the walls.

His sister Shasha, fierce and protective at 12, watches over him as their father David unravels—gripped by paranoia that borders on psychosis. But the real threat may be their charming neighbor, Tom "The Beast" Wilmington, who is actually a government spy posing as a friendly face.

As the rain pounds against the roof and the walls begin to whisper, the family must confront the shadows of their past before they consume everything. Jesse becomes the unexpected bridge between two worlds, and only he can hear what others cannot.

Our Vision

We aim to create a cinematic experience that blends psychological horror with intimate family drama. The film will use innovative sound design, practical effects, and atmospheric cinematography to create a world where the line between reality and nightmare blur.
